## Business English I - Project Documentation

### 1. User Stories
* **As a Patient**, I want to log into the app securely so that my personal health data remains protected.
* **As a Patient**, I want to view my prescribed GPR exercise plan with videos and images so that I can perform them correctly at home.
* **As a Physiotherapist (Maya)**, I want to automate the registration of new patients, so I don't have to fill out the same forms manually every time.
* **As a Physiotherapist (Maya)**, I want to send automated reminders and exercise sets to eliminate repetitive manual tasks and improve my time management.
<br>

### 2. Product Pitch (Short Version)
> "Hello, we are developing the Maya System.
> 
> Currently, Maya Yoshiko Yamamoto faces a major productivity bottleneck: every single process is done manually. From registration to exercise follow-ups, she deals with repetitive tasks that lead to management difficulties and data fragmentation.
> 
> Our solution directly tackles this by automating the clinical workflow. We provide an integrated ecosystem where the Web Module handles the heavy administrative lifting—like automated reporting and patient tracking—while the Mobile App ensures the patient stays on track. We are turning a manual, time-consuming routine into a streamlined, digital operation."

<br>

### 3. Short Product Documentation
* **Project Name:** Maya System - Clinical GPR Support.
* **Problem:** High operational overhead due to 100% manual processes. This causes repetitive work, difficulty in managing multiple patients, and lack of real-time progress tracking.
* **Solution:** A digital transformation tool that automates manual workflows. The system centralizes patient data, automates exercise prescriptions through a pre-set database, and provides digital reports, allowing Maya to focus on treatment rather than paperwork.
* **Main Features:** Automated patient onboarding, digital exercise library, and mobile visualization for patients.
* **Technologies:** Java/Android Studio (Mobile), React/Next.js (Web), and SQL Database (Backend).
